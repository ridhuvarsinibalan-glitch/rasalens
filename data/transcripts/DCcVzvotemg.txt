00:00 Authentic Hyderabadi Chicken Dum Biryani recipe by Chef Ranveer Brar.
00:30 Marinating the chicken: combine yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric, biryani masala, chopped mint, and golden fried onions (birista).
02:15 Allow the chicken to marinate for at least 1 hour for maximum flavor absorption.
04:00 For the rice: boil aged long-grain basmati rice in salted water infused with star anise, cloves, cinnamon, and shahi jeera until 70 percent cooked.
06:30 In a heavy bottom pot or handi, place the raw marinated chicken in an even layer.
08:00 Layer the fragrant parboiled rice over the chicken.
09:30 Drizzle saffron-infused warm milk, desi ghee, rose water, mint leaves, and crisp fried onions over the rice.
11:00 Seal the pot tightly with dough and lid to trap all the steam.
12:30 Cook on high flame for 5 minutes, then place on a heavy tawa on low heat for 25 minutes of slow dum cooking.
14:45 Let it rest for 10 minutes before gently opening and fluffing the layers.
15:42 Serve hot with cold onion cucumber raita and mirchi ka salan.
