01:10 Namaste, I am Chef Ranveer Brar and today we are making the quintessential restaurant style Paneer Butter Masala.
01:30 First, take a heavy-bottomed pan and add 2 tablespoons of butter and 1 tablespoon of oil.
01:45 Add green cardamom, cloves, cinnamon stick, and a bay leaf.
02:10 Add finely chopped onions and sauté until translucent.
02:40 Add fresh ginger garlic paste and sauté until the raw aroma dissipates.
03:15 Add rich red tomato puree, soaked cashew paste, Kashmiri red chili powder, and turmeric.
04:00 Cook on medium flame until the oil releases and separates from the sides of the pan.
05:30 Blend the masala into a velvety smooth makhani gravy.
06:15 Add fresh soft paneer cubes into the simmering gravy.
07:00 Finish with crushed kasuri methi, aromatic garam masala, honey, and fresh dairy cream.
07:45 Simmer gently for 3 to 4 minutes on low flame.
08:20 Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve piping hot with garlic naan or basmati rice.
