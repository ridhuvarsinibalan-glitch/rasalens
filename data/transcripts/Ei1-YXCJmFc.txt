00:00 Welcome to Ranveer Brar's kitchen. Today we are making the ultimate homestyle Chicken Curry.
00:15 Heat mustard oil in a heavy bottom kadai until smoking hot.
00:45 Add bay leaf, green cardamom, cloves, and cinnamon stick.
01:10 Add finely sliced onions and sauté on medium heat until golden brown.
02:00 Add fresh ginger-garlic paste and sauté until raw aroma disappears.
02:45 Stir in turmeric, Kashmiri red chili powder, coriander powder, and salt.
03:30 Add fresh tomato puree and cook until oil begins to separate from masala.
04:15 Add cleaned bone-in chicken pieces and roast on high flame for 5 to 7 minutes.
06:00 Pour hot water according to desired gravy consistency.
07:30 Cover with lid and simmer on low heat for 15 to 20 minutes until chicken is tender.
10:00 Finish with freshly crushed garam masala, kasuri methi, and fresh coriander leaves.
11:24 Serve hot with steamed basmati rice or hot rotis.
