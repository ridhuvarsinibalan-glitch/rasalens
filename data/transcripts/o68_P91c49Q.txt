06:00 Lift the al dente pasta directly from the boiling water into the pan so it absorbs the flavors.
07:30 Toss the pasta vigorously with the sauce to coat every strand.
08:45 Finish with fresh basil leaves, cracked black pepper, sea salt, and a squeeze of lemon juice.
09:30 Grate generous amounts of fresh Parmesan cheese over the top.
