00:45 Today we are making authentic Mexican street tacos with marinated carne asada.
01:10 Marinate the flank steak with ground cumin, Mexican chili powder, fresh lime juice, crushed garlic, and sea salt.
02:00 Let it sit in the marinade for at least 30 minutes to tenderize.
03:15 Heat a seasoned cast iron skillet over high heat with avocado oil until smoking.
04:00 Sear the steak for 3 to 4 minutes per side until you get a deep char and medium-rare center.
05:15 Rest the meat on a cutting board, then chop finely against the grain.
06:00 Warm the small corn tortillas directly on the comal or open flame.
06:45 Double-layer the tortillas, pile on the juicy steak, and top with diced white onion and chopped cilantro.
07:30 Squeeze fresh lime juice, drizzle roasted salsa verde, and serve immediately.
