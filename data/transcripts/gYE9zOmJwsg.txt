00:00 Welcome to Ranveer Brar's kitchen. Today we are making authentic Lucknowi Vegetable Dum Biryani.
00:30 For the vegetable marinade: combine carrots, cauliflower florets, French beans, green peas, and paneer cubes in thick yogurt.
01:45 Add ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, Kashmiri red chili powder, coriander powder, shahi jeera, and javitri elaichi powder.
03:00 Let the vegetables marinate for 20 minutes so they absorb the aromatic spices.
04:30 For the rice: boil aged long-grain basmati rice with whole spices (bay leaf, black cardamom, javitri, cloves, black peppercorns) until 70% done.
06:15 In a heavy-bottomed handi, layer the marinated vegetables at the base.
08:00 Cover with the fragrant parboiled basmati rice.
09:15 Drizzle saffron-infused warm milk, desi ghee, kewra water, rose water, mint leaves, and crisp fried onions (birista).
11:00 Seal the handi tightly with dough and lid.
12:30 Cook on slow dum heat for 25 to 30 minutes so the vegetables cook to tender perfection in their own steam.
14:15 Serve piping hot with cool garlic burani raita.
