00:00 Welcome back! Today we are making street style Veg Hakka Noodles and Chowmein with Chef Ranveer Brar.
00:45 Step one: boil the noodles in plenty of boiling salted water until 80 percent done. Do not overcook.
01:30 Drain immediately, rinse in cold tap water, and toss with a tablespoon of cooking oil so they stay separate and bouncy.
02:45 Get your wok piping hot. Add 2 tablespoons of oil and swirl around the edges.
03:30 Add finely chopped garlic, ginger, and slit green chilies. Toss on high heat for 30 seconds.
04:15 Add thinly sliced onions, shredded green cabbage, julienned carrots, and bell peppers. Stir fry on screaming high flame for 1 minute to keep them crisp.
06:00 Add the cooled boiled noodles straight into the sizzling wok.
07:15 Season with dark soy sauce, green chili sauce, a splash of white vinegar, crushed black pepper, and salt.
08:30 Toss the wok continuously over high flame so the noodles get that smoky wok hei flavor.
10:45 Throw in a handful of chopped spring onion greens.
12:18 Serve hot with chili vinegar or Schezwan sauce!
